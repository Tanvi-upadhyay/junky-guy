FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Have questions about our services? We've answered the most common ones below.

What items do you remove?

We remove furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, yard waste, construction debris (wood, drywall, tile), office equipment, hot tubs, sheds, and general household junk. We cannot haul hazardous materials, chemicals, wet paint, asbestos, or tires.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes! We offer same-day and next-day scheduling across New York, subject to crew availability. Contact us early in the day for the best chance of same-day service. In many cases, our team can arrive within just a few hours of your call.

How much does junk removal cost?

Pricing is volume-based — you only pay for the space your items take up in our truck. Single items start as low as $49, while full-truck loads vary depending on volume and location. We always confirm the price on-site before starting any work, with no hidden fees.

Do you recycle items?

Absolutely. We are committed to eco-responsible disposal. Every load is sorted — usable items are donated to local charities and shelters, recyclables go to appropriate facilities, and only what can't be repurposed goes to disposal. We aim to divert as much waste from landfills as possible.

Do I need to move items outside?

No — that's the whole point. Our professional crew enters your home, apartment, office, or any space and handles all the heavy lifting, carrying, and cleanup themselves. You don't need to move anything. Just show us what needs to go.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes, always. You can request a free estimate online, send us photos of your items, or call us directly. Our on-site estimate is confirmed with you before any work begins — there are never any surprise charges after the fact.
