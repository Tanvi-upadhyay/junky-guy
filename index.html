FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Have questions about our junk removal process? Find answers to our most common inquiries below.

Are you licensed and insured in New Jersey?

Yes, Junky Guy is fully licensed and insured across New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania. Our professional truck teams are covered by comprehensive liability and workers' compensation insurance to guarantee absolute safety and protection for your property.

What areas do you serve?

We provide full-service residential and commercial junk hauling across North, Central, and South Jersey, New York City (and its surrounding suburbs), and the Greater Philadelphia metro area.

Do you offer free estimates?

Absolutely. We provide free, transparent, no-obligation estimates. You can request a quote online, send us a photo of the items, or call us directly. Our team always confirms the final volume-based price on-site before starting any haul.

How quickly can I schedule service?

We offer fast same-day and next-day scheduling. Depending on the time of day you contact us, our local truck teams can often arrive at your location within just a few hours.

What types of properties do you service?

We work with single-family houses, apartments, condos, offices, retail spaces, warehouses, construction sites, estate cleanups, and property management turnovers.

Do you recycle or donate items?

Yes. We sort through every load to separate items that can be recycled or donated to local shelters and charities (like furniture, working appliances, and clothes). We are dedicated to keeping as much waste out of local landfills as possible.

What types of junk do you remove?

We remove furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics, yard waste, remodeling debris (wood, drywall, tile), and general trash. Please note: we cannot haul away hazardous materials, chemicals, wet paint, or tires.
